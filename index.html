About

Who I am and what I work with.

I'm a science student from Ghana obsessed with robotics, drones and embedded systems. Building machines that physically interact with the world is what gets me out of bed in the morning.

I see robots as real solutions to real-world problems. From life-saving drones to smart IoT safety devices, every project I take on is built with a purpose — and most of them start because something around me needed fixing.

Beyond building, I teach. I run TechCamp Boot Camp, where students learn practical electronics, programming and cybersecurity hands-on. I'm working toward a Mechatronics Engineering degree, and I'm always open to mentors and collaborators who share a vision for an automated future.
